More of Home, at a glance

EVERY SCENE APPROVED (2026-11-28)

Android’s installed widget set, redrawn at the density of the supplied 0.11 X4 photos: compact half-width values, one hero, four-cover shelf rows and three-book lists. Pro and Classic use identical footprints. All values, cover art and titles here are invented; the private photos are not embedded.

Exact Android inventory, main sizes and acceptance notes · Firmware gallery. This is design work only; no firmware implementation, pin change, removed Android feature or approval.

Physical size key · 480 × 800 design viewport

One compact unit is 224 × 108 px; full width is 456 px with an 8 px gutter. Six rows fit between the 64 px header and 42 px footer. A 2×2 hero or shelf is 456 × 224 px, not half a screen. These are proposed firmware grid footprints, not changes to Android’s size registry.

All (n) › is always in the header’s top-right. Shelf count means the known saved source total; a preview never invents it. Larger views are isolated at the end as optional extras.
