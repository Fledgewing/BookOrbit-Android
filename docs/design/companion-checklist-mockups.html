Companion setup checklist

ROADMAP 10a, Android. Today's Setup page is one flat list of up to 22 rows of equal weight. The redesign puts a summary first ("All set" or "2 things need you"), expands only what needs you, folds finished rows into one line per group and uses plain words. Every row that is off, skipped or not done yet says what doesn't work without it, in both Full and Add-on mode. Nothing has been built. I settled every reversible choice myself; the design doc lists them with their reasons.

Decisions
  1. ✓
    No one-way doors.

    All of it is wording and layout. Saved skips and snoozes keep their ids, so relabelling resets nothing.

  2. ✓
    "What you lose without it" shows in both modes

    Your ruling: every optional or skipped row says "Without it: …", and every required row that isn't done says "Until then: …". One table holds the lines for both modes, and a test fails on any row without one.

What drove the design
  • On a set-up Boox Page, 12 "done" rows pushed the one open row (BookSync's battery setting) halfway down. Finished rows now take one line per group.
  • Battery was three rows with one fix. It's now one row, with a detail page for each app.
  • "(NET_UP)", "(device admin)" and "Always-on on" are internals. They move to Details, which has a Copy for support button.
  • The label said what to do ("Set BookSync as Home app") and "done" sat on the right. Labels are now nouns, with the state on the line below and one verb on the button.
  • On the Boox, BookSync's battery setting is already the way BookSync needs it, and the reader doesn't let anyone change it. That's a new state, "Set by the reader", and it counts as done.
  • E-ink: no animation, no spinner and no timed redraws. Expanding a group takes one redraw, and state is shown by glyph and words, never by grey alone.

Both are the same Boox Page in the same state: only BookSync's battery setting is open. Drawn at 3:4, which is the shape of all three readers (M8C 1404 × 1872, Boox Page and Go Color 7 1264 × 1680), at about 0.28 scale.

The five states

Black and white only. "Set by the reader" counts as done. Off or skipped rows and rows still checking are never counted and never prompt.

Ongoing: all set, needs you, a group, battery

The summary always comes first. Each open row sits under Needs you, expanded, with what doesn't work until it's fixed and one verb. Tap a group to open it; only one is open at a time.

Modes, skipped steps and kids' readers

Full and Add-on use the same page. Add-on counts only its four required rows and keeps its permissions under Optional. Both modes show "Without it: …" on every optional or skipped row.

First run, updates and details

First run is this same page with a Start button that runs the existing step-by-step walk. A step that arrives with an update gets a "New in" tag.
