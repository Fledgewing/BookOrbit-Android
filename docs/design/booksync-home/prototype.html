Revision 4 · clickable design prototype · synthetic data · no network, files, or real downloads

Your Home, built one widget at a time

The first release has Continue reading, Shelf and Browse links. Later widgets are visible as preview examples here; the picker labels their release status. Turn off “Preview later widgets” to try the starter set.

Tap a book to open or download it. Hold a book for its actions. On X4 Classic, Prev/Next moves focus, Select activates it, and More opens actions.

Choose More → Edit layout to add, remove, move and resize widgets. Moves jump to grid cells, with no animation. X4 Select grabs a tile; More cycles the movement/size axis; Prev/Next steps it.
